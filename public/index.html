The Efficiency Paradox

techjourneyman published on 2026-08-19 included in Sci-Fi Ai Governance

Dr. Marcus Chen had spent three years building the case, and now it sat compiled in a single folder on his laptop: California, if it seceded tomorrow, would be the second-richest nation on Earth, trailing only the United States itself. Not oil. Not tourism. Governance. Fifteen years ago, the state had folded artificial intelligence into the machinery of administration — permitting, budgeting, infrastructure routing, even judicial scheduling — and the results were undeniable. Wait times for permits had dropped from months to hours. The state budget, once a battlefield of pork and gridlock, balanced itself with surgical precision. Roads got built. Wildfires got predicted and starved of fuel before they could spread. It was, by every metric Marcus could measure, the most efficient government in human history.
